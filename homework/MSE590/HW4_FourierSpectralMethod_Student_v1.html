Fourier spectral method for Cahn-Hilliard equation#

In this assignment, we will use FFT to simulation spinodal decomposition. The giverning equation is Cahn-Hilliard equation, which we have learned in Lab 6:

\[\frac{\partial \phi}{\partial t} = M \nabla^2 \mu = M \nabla^2 \bigg( \frac{\partial f}{\partial \phi} - \varepsilon^2 \nabla^2 \phi \bigg),\]

where the chemical potential for the bulk phase is

\[\frac{\partial f}{\partial \phi} = f'(\phi) = \Omega \big(1-2\phi \big) + \ln \phi - \ln \big(1-\phi \big).\]

The simulation is set up as in Lab 6. The cell below will set the relevant conditions.

The Fourier transform of Cahn-Hilliard equation is

\[F\bigg[\frac{\phi^{(n+1)}-\phi^{(n)}}{\Delta t} \bigg]= M F\big[\nabla^2 \mu\big]= M F \big[ \nabla^2 \big(f'(\phi^{(n)}) - \varepsilon^2 \nabla^2 \phi^{(n)} \big) \big] \Longrightarrow F\big[\phi^{(n+1)}\big] = F\big[\phi^{(n)}\big] - \Delta t M \omega^2 F \big[ f'(\phi^{(n)}) - \varepsilon^2 \nabla^2 \phi^{(n)} \big],\]

which can be rewritten to

\[ F\big[\phi^{(n+1)}\big] = F\big[\phi^{(n)}\big] - \Delta t M \omega^2 \big\{F \big[ f'(\phi^{(n)}) \big] + \varepsilon^2 \omega^2 F\big[ \phi^{(n)} \big] \big\}.\]

So, the workflow will be

  1. Set up reciprocal basis, \(\omega\).

  2. Take fft2 of \(\phi\) to get \(F[\phi]\), multiply with \(-\omega^2\).

  3. Calculate the value of \(f'(\phi)\).

  4. Take fft2 of \(f'\) to get \(F[f']\),

  5. Compute \(F[\mu]\) by \(F[f']-\varepsilon^2(-\omega^2F[\phi])\), where \(\mu = f' - \varepsilon^2 \nabla^2 \phi\).

  6. Muliply \(-\omega^2\) to obtain \(-\omega^2 F[\mu]\), which is essentially \(F[\nabla^2 \mu]\).

  7. Time stepping with quantities in the reciprocal space: \(F[\phi^{(n+1)}] = F[\phi^{(n)}] -\Delta t M \omega^2 F[\mu^{(n)}]\).

  8. After \(\phi\) in Fourier space is updated, use ifft to get its values in the real space because it’s needed for computing the values of \(f'(\phi)\).
