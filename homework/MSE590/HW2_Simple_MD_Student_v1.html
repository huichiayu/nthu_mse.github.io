Part 1. A closed system#

You can follow the procedures below to complete your code.

  1. When an atom hits a wall, it bounces back. So, using Euler method and west wall as example, the atoms’ \(x\) position will be updated using the rule

\( x_i(t+\Delta t) = x_i(t) + v_i(t) \Delta t\)

\(\text{if}~~x_i(t+\Delta t) > 0,~~x_i(t+\Delta t) = 0 - x_i(t+\Delta t).\)

See the figure for illustation.

You should be able to figure out the bounce-back conditions needed for the other 3 walls.

  1. since there is no periodic boundary condition, we no longer need to consider the imaging atoms in the periodically repeated cells when calculating inter-atom distances.

  2. Let’s assume that the atoms interacting with walls can also be described by Lennard-Jones Potential, but with a smaller interaction strength (\(\epsilon_W = 0.1\)). The LJ potential will be a function of the distance between the atom and wall. Note that we decrease the cutoff radius to \(2.5\times r_m\) in this work for convenience.
