Part 1. Plot the analytical solution of 1D diffusion equation.#

\[C_{ana} = \sqrt{\frac{t_0}{t}} \exp\bigg( - \frac{x^2}{4Dt}\bigg).\]

Part 2. Finite different method#

Part 2.1. Simulate diffusion in 1D using finite difference methods.#

We will discretize the domain to \(N\) pieces as in the figure below. The boundary points are “0” and “\(N-1\)”. We will assume periodic boundary conditions.

\(~\)

The finite difference stencil using Euler time scheme and central differencing in space for

\[\frac{\partial C}{\partial t} = D \frac{\partial C^2}{\partial x^2}\]

is

\[ \frac{C_j^{(n+1)}-C_j^{(n)}}{\Delta t} = D \frac{C_{j-1}^{(n)} -2 C_{j}^{(n)} + C_{j+1}^{(n)}}{\Delta x^2} ~\Longrightarrow~C_j^{(n+1)} = C_j^{(n)} + \frac{ \Delta t D}{\Delta x^2} \big(C_{j-1}^{(n)} -2 C_{j}^{(n)} + C_{j+1}^{(n)} \big),\]

where superscript \((n)\) indicates time step and subscript \(j\) indicates the label of grid point. Complete the code below.
